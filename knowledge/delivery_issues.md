# Delivery problems

## Marked as delivered but not received
First check with neighbours, your building's reception and any safe place you agreed with the carrier, and wait until the end of the next working day: drivers sometimes scan a parcel as delivered slightly early. If it still has not turned up, reply with your tracking number and we will open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations take up to 10 working days.

## Damaged parcel
Please keep the parcel and all packaging, and send us photos of the outside of the box, the shipping label and the damaged items within 7 days of delivery. We forward the claim to the carrier. Whether you are compensated, and how much, depends on the carrier's assessment and the shop's insurance, so we cannot promise an outcome in advance. For a replacement or refund of the item itself, please also contact the shop you bought from.

## Lost parcel
A parcel counts as lost when the investigation with the carrier confirms it, usually within 10 working days. The shop that sent the parcel is your contract partner and will arrange a replacement or refund once the parcel is confirmed lost.

## Late delivery
Estimated delivery dates are estimates from the carrier, not guarantees. Domestic parcels usually arrive within 1-2 working days and EU parcels within 2-5 working days. Delays are more common around holidays and in December.

## Missed delivery
After a failed attempt the carrier either tries again the next working day or leaves the parcel at the nearest pickup point. The tracking page shows which pickup point. Parcels are kept for 7 days before being returned to the shop.
