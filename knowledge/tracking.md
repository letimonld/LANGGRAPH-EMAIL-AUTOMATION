# Tracking your parcel

## Where to find your tracking number
Your tracking number is in the shipping confirmation email from the shop. Parcelo tracking numbers look like PCL123456789NL: the letters PCL, nine digits and a two-letter country code. Parcels handed to a partner carrier may use that carrier's format instead.

## How often tracking updates
Tracking updates each time the parcel is scanned: at pickup, at each sorting centre, when it is out for delivery and on delivery. Between scans there can be no update for up to 24 hours on domestic routes and up to 72 hours on international routes. This is normal and does not mean the parcel is lost.

## Status meanings
- Announced: the shop created a label but the carrier has not picked the parcel up yet.
- In transit: the parcel is moving between sorting centres.
- Out for delivery: the driver has the parcel and will deliver today.
- Delivery attempted: nobody was home; the carrier will try again the next working day or leave it at a pickup point.
- At pickup point: collect it with your tracking number and ID within 7 days.
- Delivered: the carrier recorded the delivery.

## Parcel shows no movement
If the status stays "Announced" for more than 3 working days, the shop may not have handed the parcel over yet. Please contact the shop first; they can check with us directly. If the parcel was already in transit and has not moved for more than 5 working days, reply to this email and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Track online
You can follow your parcel at https://track.parcelo.example by entering the tracking number and your postcode.
