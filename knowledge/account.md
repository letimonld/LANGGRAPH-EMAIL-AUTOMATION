# Account and contact

## Contact and opening hours
The support team answers email on working days from 08:00 to 18:00 CET, usually within one working day.

## Personal data requests
Requests to access or delete personal data under the GDPR are handled by our privacy team. Customers can write to privacy@parcelo.example.

## Who to contact: Parcelo or the shop
Parcelo transports parcels on behalf of online shops. For questions about the order itself - items, payment, invoices, refunds and replacements - please contact the shop. For questions about the transport - tracking, delivery, pickup points - you can contact us.
