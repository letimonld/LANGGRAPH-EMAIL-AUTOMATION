# Customs and duties

## Shipping outside the EU
Parcels leaving the EU, including to the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway, need a customs declaration. Parcelo creates it automatically from the item descriptions, HS codes and values the shop enters.

## Import duties and VAT for customers
When a parcel enters a country outside the EU, local import VAT and duties may be charged to the recipient on delivery, unless the shop shipped DDP (delivered duty paid). For parcels sent into the EU from outside, the shop may have charged VAT at checkout under the IOSS scheme.

## Parcel held by customs
Customs may hold a parcel to check its contents or documents. Clearance usually takes 1-5 working days. If customs needs an invoice or more information, the carrier contacts the recipient or the shop directly. We cannot speed up customs clearance.
