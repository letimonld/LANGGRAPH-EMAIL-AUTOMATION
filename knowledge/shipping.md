# Shipping with Parcelo

## Countries we ship to
Parcelo ships from the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Austria and the United Kingdom to more than 100 countries through partner carriers such as PostNL, DHL, DPD, UPS and GLS.

## Shipping prices for shops
Prices depend on the plan, the carrier, the weight and the destination. Shops see the exact price per label in their Parcelo account before buying it. The plans and their included features are listed at https://parcelo.example/pricing. We cannot quote individual prices by email.

## Parcel size and weight limits
Most carriers accept parcels up to 31.5 kg and 175 cm on the longest side. Exact limits differ per carrier and service and are shown when creating a label.

## Packaging tips
Use a sturdy box, fill empty space so items cannot move, and remove or cover old labels and barcodes. Liquids must be sealed and packed in a plastic bag.

## Prohibited items
Lithium batteries shipped on their own, aerosols, flammable liquids, weapons, and perishable food are not accepted by most carriers. The full list is at https://parcelo.example/prohibited-items.

## Printing labels
Labels can be printed on A4 or on a 4x6 inch label printer from the Parcelo account. If a label does not print, check the printer settings in Settings > Printing.
