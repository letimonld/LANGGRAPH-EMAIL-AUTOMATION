# Returns and refunds

## How to return a parcel
Returns are arranged through the shop you bought from. Many shops use the Parcelo return portal at https://returns.parcelo.example where you enter your order number and email address, choose a reason and print a return label or get a QR code to show at a drop-off point.

## Return window
The return window is set by the shop, usually 14 or 30 days after delivery. Within the EU you have the legal right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days of receiving it.

## Return shipping costs
Whether return shipping is free depends on the shop's policy. The return portal shows the price before you confirm.

## Refunds
Parcelo transports the parcel; the refund is paid by the shop. Shops usually refund within 14 days after the return parcel is delivered back to them. You can see in tracking when your return parcel has been delivered to the shop. If the refund has not arrived 14 days after that, please contact the shop.
