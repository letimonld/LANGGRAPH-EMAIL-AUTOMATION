# Changing a delivery

## Change the delivery address
The address can only be changed before the parcel is in transit, and only by the shop or by the recipient through the tracking page at https://track.parcelo.example. Once a parcel is in transit, the address can no longer be changed, but you can often redirect it to a pickup point.

## Redirect to a pickup point
On the tracking page choose "Deliver to pickup point" while the status is "Announced" or "In transit". This is free and available in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and France.

## Reschedule delivery
On the tracking page you can choose another delivery day when the carrier offers it. Not every carrier supports rescheduling.

## Verification
For your security we only make changes requested from the email address or phone number the shop gave us for the order.
